and procedural sound effects. It does not relicense third-party artwork or
trademarks.

- The website's historical Apple wallpaper, application icons, and Apple mark
  retain their original rights. Sources and attribution are recorded in
  [website/ASSET-SOURCES.md](website/ASSET-SOURCES.md).
- The GitHub shortcut incorporates GitHub's Octicons mark under the
  [included MIT notice](website/dist/assets/Octicons-LICENSE.txt). GitHub's name
  and logo remain GitHub trademarks.
- Sound effects were authored with Earshot. Its MIT notice is included in
  [Resources/Sounds/Earshot-LICENSE.txt](Resources/Sounds/Earshot-LICENSE.txt)
  and alongside the website's WAV files. See
  [sound-design/README.md](sound-design/README.md) for provenance.
- Build and development dependencies are referenced in their package manifests;
  their code is not vendored into this repository.
